## Local adaptations

The family strips acquisition/remote resolution and prints no model logs on stdout. NativeModel adapters use IntegrationKit's worker, media decoding, cancellation and streamed precision loader. Checkpoint names/permutations are explicit in each model's `gate/header-map.json`; no name-only architecture aliases substitute a different model graph. All learned tensors, including watermark parameters and unused auxiliary speaker heads, remain inventoried. Computed positional tables, filterbanks and shared-object aliases are excluded explicitly rather than treated as learned parameters.

FP16 is the declared serving base; source BF16/FP32 is accurately pinned, with a cast recipe. All eligible group-64 matrices use the selected int8/int4 tier, including the retired Qwen port's audio encoder and decoder (the VellaSTT Qwen3-ASR keeps its audio tower half, Vella's recipe), T5, and both recurrent projections. Source `Wx`/`Wh` tensors remain half in Get's generic flat snapshot and are packed by the ordinary tier loader after their owning Linear mapping at Load. Spatial convolutions, norms and matrices whose input width is not divisible by 64 stay half. Quantized quality is unqualified.

Smart Turn follows the documented Whisper frontend: left padding, last-eight-second truncation, variance epsilon and periodic Hann. Demucs computes normalization statistics and FFT host/DSP intermediates in Float32, while learned weights and network activations are half; half variance reduction otherwise overflowed on the random tone. Nemotron's SDPA mask is cast to the attention dtype. Borrowed source formatting is normalized to repository lint rules while residual assignments avoid MLXArray's mutating `+=` alias behavior.

SAM retains the source port’s ten-second/three-second-overlap long-audio path and temporal-anchor preprocessing. Anchors are limited to a single ten-second clip because the source port explicitly rejects chunked anchors. Its vision encoder is absent upstream and is not advertised. The pipeline header maps include the separately pinned T5 encoder and explicitly ignore its unused decoder.

Model-specific source revisions, exact file byte counts/hashes, supported operation schemas and outstanding contracts are in the model folders and the integration handoff. There are no trained-weight or numerical-fidelity claims in the random-graph checks.

## Shared-host prerequisites

These adapters do not expand the shared fetcher/loader or HTTP/MCP files. FP32/BF16 sources use the generic Get-time FP16 normalization; each header map records its per-tensor cast plan. SAM Audio (`cast:audio-sam-v1`/`native:audio-sam-v1`; Large from Meta's original `checkpoint.pt` through `cast:audio-sam-v2`, which applies mlx-audio's `sam_audio` sanitize names and load-time convolution layouts tensor by tensor) and Community-1 (`cast:audio-community1-v1`/`audio-community1-v1`) declare model-owned base conversions that depend on the shared base-cast staging fix (Get must stage the normalized source rather than recurse into its own FP16 base). SAM's base conversion reads the verified T5-base asset and writes its encoder into the snapshot; Get-time half-only storage of the raw asset itself is the shared derived-asset item. Confucius `transcribe_session` and Nemotron `diarize_session` are declared operations; the Realtime WebSocket bridges append, commit and finish to these sessions (docs/API.md, Live transcription sessions). SAM small/large, HTDemucs-ft and Community-1 dispatch by the folder `architecture` key; HTDemucs reads its shipped `htdemucs_ft_config.json`. None of these shared files are edited by the audio integrator.
