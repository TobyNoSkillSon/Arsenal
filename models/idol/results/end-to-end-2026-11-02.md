# IDOL end to end with the trained weights (2 Nov 2026, M5 Max, measured; the GPU was shared with another task)

Four of IDOL's own gallery cutouts (the left halves of `assets/gallery/*.webp`), defaults (50 steps, guidance 3,
262,144 Gaussians, seed 42), through `IdolPipeline` (`IdolEndToEndTests.trainedModelMakesSplats`), each rendered from
the estimated camera direction and three quarter turns, then inspected.

| Picture | Time | Peak MLX memory | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| armchair | 128–139 s | 6.8 GB | Matches the picture from its direction; deeper than wide from the side (see below) |
| grandma figurine | 129 s | 6.9 GB | Clean from all four sides: face, glasses, bun, scarf, knitting needles |
| stained-glass lamp | 126 s | 6.9 GB | Clean from all four sides; the shade pattern continues round the back |
| axolotl | 115 s | 6.9 GB | Clean front; the tail runs long along the line of sight |

The renders are in the task's lab folder (`lab/idol/e2e/out`), not in git.

idol-sdk's own pipeline on MPS takes about 85–92 s for the same picture on the same shared Mac (optimize off; not a
benchmark, the GPU was in use).

Depth along the line of sight over width across it, armchair (`Workers/Geometry/scripts` capture plus a percentile
ratio of the opaque Gaussians): idol-sdk seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 42: 1.05, 1.07, 1.51, 1.05, 1.07. Arsenal seeds 1, 2, 3, 42:
1.49, 1.03, 1.45, 1.51, and seed 7 (idol-sdk's image tokens) about 1.06. So idol-sdk itself makes the stretched chair on
some seeds. Started from idol-sdk's noise and tokens, Arsenal's sampler reproduces idol-sdk's latent (cosine 0.99994),
and the stretch follows the noise draw, not the tokens. Not settled: whether the stretched share really differs (3 of 5
here against 1 of 5 there is within chance at this size); twenty seeds each would settle it.
