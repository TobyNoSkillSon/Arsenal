Stable Audio 3 in Vireo

Code
- Sources/VireoEngine/Vendor/StableAudio3/{Conditioner,DiTMedium,FlowMatchingSampler,SAMELDecoder,
  T5GemmaEncoder,UnigramTokenizer,Configuration,StableAudio3,StableAudio3WeightLoading,
  SentencePieceModel}.swift are adapted from soniqo/speech-swift
  (https://github.com/soniqo/speech-swift, commit 2af9c6c9f415ab1ad63fd8a5dc4ed0192f8263de,
  targets StableAudio3MusicGen and AudioCommon), Copyright 2025 Ivan Digital, Apache License 2.0
  (speech-swift-Apache-2.0.txt). Modified for Vireo: official F16 .npz loading, strict key checks,
  plain Linear layers with optional quantization, configurable DiT shape, input-length local
  conditioning, fp32 timestep, key(seed) noise, a byte-exact SentencePiece BPE tokenizer,
  cancellation. Each modified file says what changed.
- Behaviour was checked against Stability AI's MLX runtime
  (https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-3/tree/main/optimized/mlx, MIT licence).
  Sources/VireoEngine/Vendor/StableAudio3/SAMESDecoder.swift (the SAME-S decoder of the small
  models) and the plain-attention path of DiTMedium.swift (the small DiTs) are Swift ports of its
  models/defs/same_s_decoder.py and models/defs/dit_mlx.py (commit 779434a), Copyright (c) 2026
  Stability AI, MIT licence (StableAudio3-MLX-MIT.txt). Nothing else from it is included beyond the
  constants and structure it documents.
