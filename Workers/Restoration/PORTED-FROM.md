# Restoration sources

- RIFENative: https://github.com/xocialize/rife-mlx-swift at `f926ca03a910f53a34cd025f03c0ae20e24d63a9`. Imported `Sources/RIFEMLX/{IFNet,Ops,RIFEModel}.swift`. MIT, notice in `LICENSE-upstream-RIFENative`. Checkpoint layout: mlx-community/RIFE-4.25, NHWC spatial kernels, no renamed parameters.
- SeedVR2Native: https://github.com/xocialize/seedvr2-mlx-swift at `1dfa161ba9522bdf1295015fd0b00209a4e30093`. Imported Config, Models/Transformer, Models/VAE and Pipeline/{ColorCorrect,LatentCreator,Scheduler}. Excluded upstream network download helpers, bulk weight loader, application/CLI and conflicting package graph. Checkpoint layout: mlx-community/SeedVR2-3B-mlx. Upstream has SPDX MIT source headers and an MIT statement in README but no standalone LICENSE; `LICENSE-upstream-SeedVR2Native` preserves that fact.
- DDColorNative: https://github.com/xocialize/mlx-ddcolor-swift at `0bf684108316260a42c5c59db52e936c6a9336b6`. Imported `Sources/DDColor/{DDColorModel,DDColorOps,Lab}.swift`; excluded its engine package, Hub download, CLI smokes and CG resize. MIT, notice in `LICENSE-upstream-DDColorNative`. Checkpoint layout: mlx-community/DDColor-modelscope-fp16 (575 tensors, NHWC, spectral norm resolved). Local changes: MLX fused layer norm (float32 accumulation; the manual layer norm overflows at FP16) and precise softmax, a fixed tensor inventory (`DDColorWeights`), and the makers' own `ddcolor/pipeline.py` around the network (cv2 bilinear resize to the input size, nearest ab resize, rounding) instead of CoreGraphics resampling.
- LaMaNative: https://github.com/xocialize/mlx-lama-swift at `f085b8f4fddf6320a347e0e3ecb28fad20959b53`. Imported the Big-LaMa generator from `Sources/LaMa/{LaMaModel,LaMaOps}.swift`; excluded MI-GAN, the engine package, Hub download and the MLXExactConv dependency. MIT, notice in `LICENSE-upstream-LaMaNative`. Checkpoint layout: mlx-community/LaMa-fp32 (837 FP32 tensors, NHWC). Local changes: every eval BatchNorm is folded into the convolution before it at Get (`LaMaFold`, recipe `cast:restoration-lama-v1` on the raw FP32 source), because running variances up to 4.8e5 exceed FP16 and the unfolded graph collapses at FP16 and BF16; the generator then runs at FP16 with float32 FFTs (as the makers' half path); ortho FFT normalization uses MLX's own `norm: .ortho`; the pipeline follows advimman/lama `bin/predict.py` (mask > 0, symmetric padding, truncating 8-bit output).

- GIMMNative: Arsenal's own Swift MLX port of GIMM-VFI-R from https://github.com/GSeanCDAT/GIMM-VFI at `dbc56449994a3c2e045d46fd46bed570239913f8` (`gimmvfi_r.py`, `raft/`, `modules/{fi_components,fi_utils,hyponet,coord_sampler,softsplat}.py`), checked per component against that code on MPS. S-Lab License 1.0 (non-commercial), notice in `LICENSE-upstream-GIMMNative`; its RAFT parts follow princeton-vl/RAFT (BSD-3-Clause). Checkpoint: GSean/GIMM-VFI `gimmvfi_r_arb.pt`, read at Get by IntegrationKit's shared non-executing `TorchCheckpoint` reader (`cast:restoration-gimm-vfi-r-v1`: maker names, OHWI kernels, FP16, no `num_batches_tracked` or `norm3` aliases). Precision follows the maker's autocast mixed-precision mode (FP16 convolutions and matmuls; float32 normalisation, sampling, flows and splatting), with the flow-deviation term in float32. The CuPy softsplat forward is an MLX scatter-add in float32. Local choices: frames under 64 px a side pad to 64; above about a megapixel the flow stages run at the makers' ds_factor 0.5 or 0.25.

All imported closures use Arsenal's unchanged MLX revision `901941965d82e4a216d4d117231d847d194c563d`. Formatting and narrowly scoped lint exceptions preserve upstream mathematical identifiers and avoid MLX's alias-mutating shorthand assignments. RIFE interpolation weights/timestep stay in the activation dtype. SeedVR2 VAE activation casts and the time embedding use FP16; this adaptation is unqualified on trained weights. The VAE accepts reduced channel counts only for explicit random-weight fixtures. The adapter supplies streamed NativePrecisionTier loading for transformer/VAE matrices and inventories the fixed positive embedding. The registered converter handles the original flat safetensors snapshot.

Video adapters use IntegrationKit AVFoundation frame reading/writing. RIFE retains original timestamps through uniform target-rate sampling; SeedVR2 retains timestamps through 4n+1-frame temporal batches (21 frames at 720p output, down to five at 4K), repeating the last frame to satisfy the causal VAE's 4n+1 input length, with one noise draw per clip. Local adaptations to the VAE: `Upsample3D` applies upstream `remove_head` for every temporal length (the import kept only the one-latent-frame case, so five frames decoded to eight), group norms are per frame like upstream `causal_norm_wrapper`, and `SeedVR2VAE.tiled` encodes/decodes overlapping spatial tiles blended with linear ramps (an Arsenal memory bound; upstream does not tile). Video outputs keep the source audio: `MediaBridge.keepAudio` composes the rendered video with the source's audio tracks over the rendered span and exports MP4 (passthrough, else re-encode); `AudioMuxTests` covers it. Upscaling uses Lanczos preparation, optional softness and source LAB color transfer; sizes floor to even pixels and black padding completes the multiple of 16. No upstream download path is imported.

Music is a separate family helper with its own source closure. RIFE is admitted using the model folder's explicit `architecture = "rife"`, an FP32-to-FP16 Get cast map and honest zero-matrix declarations for int8/int4 selections: its spatial-only graph remains FP16 without claiming compression.

- FlashVSRNative: https://github.com/xocialize/mlx-flashvsr-swift at `ec67024ba2939115f780d4183b4653b8a99ed1ee`, a port of OpenImagingLab/FlashVSR at `cf910c61a60733e610e9c6e8b607f80c3a6c202b`. Imported `Sources/FlashVSRMLX/{DiT,LQProjector,TCDecoder,SparseAttention,Pipeline,Weights,Preprocess}.swift`; excluded the MLXEngine package (`MLXFlashVSR`), its frame-stream-native video I/O, the smoke/gate executable and the Python oracle. Apache-2.0 (the port, FlashVSR, DiffSynth-Studio and Wan2.1; the TCDecoder derives from TAEHV, MIT), licence and NOTICE in `LICENSE-upstream-FlashVSRNative`. Checkpoint layout: mlx-community/FlashVSR-v1.1-bf16 (upstream keys verbatim, channels-last convolutions). Local changes: an SPDX line on each file, swift-format layout, and narrowly scoped SwiftLint exceptions that keep MLX's non-mutating `x = x + y` form; no numerical change. The adapter (`RestorationWorker/FlashVSRAdapter.swift`) replaces the engine package: AVFoundation frames through the port's PIL-bicubic preparation and `FlashVSRStream`, edge padding to multiples of 128 and a crop back, as the port's own driver does, with the source audio kept by `MediaBridge.keepAudio`. Get casts the BF16 files to FP16 (`cast:restoration-flashvsr`); the port's author ran BF16.
